the 75% and the 25%

Where each part of a retiree's pension comes from, why one part stopped, and what the documents say about both.

key term Settlement Class Member A retiree or beneficiary covered by the 2013 settlement.

1 · The promise

In 2013, a federal court approved a settlement in Betty Johnson v. Eloy S. Inos, Case No. 09-00023. Retirees had sued because the Government had not paid what it owed into the Retirement Fund for years, and the Fund was running out of money.

The settlement created the NMI Settlement Fund, which took over certain Retirement Fund assets. Its job is to make sure every Settlement Class Member receives at least 75% of their Full Benefits.

key term Minimum Annual Payment The amount the Government must pay the Settlement Fund each fiscal year.

2 · Where the 75% comes from

The Settlement Fund pays the guaranteed 75% from its own assets and from payments the settlement requires the NMI Government to make every year.

That required payment peaked at $45 million in FY 2018 and has fallen each year since, to $29 million in FY 2026. The Fund's status report for FY 2026 references a loan from MPLT.

3 · Where the 25% came from

The settlement guarantees 75%. The remaining 25% was paid by the Government separately. That is why it could stop without the settlement itself changing.
