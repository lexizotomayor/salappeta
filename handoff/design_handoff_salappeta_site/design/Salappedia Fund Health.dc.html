will the money last?

The short answer: yes, for the 75% — if the Government keeps paying and the investments keep earning. Here is what the two latest reports say, in plain words.

the plan: spend it down

The Fund is meant to shrink. As members pass away, less is paid out, and the Government's yearly payment gets smaller too.

By about 2048 the account is expected to be empty. From then on, the Government is expected to pay each year's benefits directly.

and your health insurance

Health insurance is not paid from the Settlement Fund. It comes from the Government's yearly budget, through its Group Health and Life Insurance program (GHLI).

Under the settlement, the Government must give retirees the same health insurance it gives full-time employees. That means every budget fight puts coverage at risk.

2026 Covered

A last-minute budget in December 2025 renewed the Aetna contract for about 7,000 retirees and employees.

2027 Not settled

The FY 2027 budget has passed the House and is now with the Senate. Lawmakers have called it a very tight budget.

Being discussed

Lawmakers and the Department of Finance have talked about moving retirees 65 and older onto Medicare as their main coverage, since insuring them on the Aetna plan is costly.

Another idea is Guam's approach, where the government reimburses retirees' Medicare premiums instead. Nothing has been decided.
