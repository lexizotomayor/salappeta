about salåppeʼta

An independent site that exists to keep the conversation going about what the Northern Mariana Islands' retirees are facing, and to put the documents behind it within everyone's reach.

Why this site exists

Thousands of retirees gave their working lives to public service in the Commonwealth. Today, what they receive depends on court orders, budget votes and fund reports that few people have the time to read.

Salåppeʼta gathers those documents in one place, explains them in plain language, and keeps a record of what changes and when, so retirees, their families and the public can follow along and keep talking about it.

It is especially meant for readers outside the system, such as family members, students, journalists and lawmakers' staff, who want to understand and discuss the issues facing retirees.

Alexie Zotomayor
Founder and editor
a note on our role

A supplement, not a replacement

Salåppeʼta does not replace the NMI Settlement Fund or its website. The Fund and the Settlement Fund Trustee's office have done many years of exemplary work for members, and nmisf.com remains the official source for notices, forms, reports and benefit questions.

This site is supplementary. It exists to keep the conversation going and to help people thresh out the topics that concern retirees.

A fair question

nmisf.com already has the documents. why another site?

It does, and it should stay the first place members go. The Fund publishes records; we help people talk about them. The two do different jobs.

The name

Salåppeʼ is the Chamorro word for money; -ta makes it ours. The name is a reminder that these pensions were built from the contributions of the people who earned them.

Not legal or financial advice. Salåppeʼta is independent of the NMI Settlement Fund, the CNMI Government and the Legislature. For decisions about your own benefits, contact the NMI Settlement Fund or a qualified adviser.
